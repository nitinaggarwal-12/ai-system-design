# PRIVACY POLICY

**Last Updated:** August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you interact with the **AI System Design: A Visual Guide to Agentic Architecture** GitHub repository and its associated web portal.

## 1. The GitHub Repository

When you visit, clone, or fork the `ai-system-design` repository, we do not collect any personal data beyond the standard analytics provided by GitHub (e.g., traffic metrics, star counts, and clone statistics). 
* Any interactions with this repository are governed by [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

## 2. The Book Web Portal

If you choose to interact with our official portal at **https://ai-system-design.up.railway.app/** (such as joining the waitlist), we collect the following information:
* **Email Address:** Collected solely for the purpose of notifying you about the book's launch, early access to chapters, or significant updates to the architectural diagrams.

## 3. How We Use and Protect Your Data

* **No Spam & No Selling:** We will never sell, rent, or share your email address with third parties or data brokers.
* **Sole Purpose:** Your email is strictly used for communication regarding the *AI System Design* book and its associated visual resources.
* **Infrastructure:** The portal is hosted securely, and your data is routed safely to a private environment.

## 4. Your Rights

You have the right to opt out of our communications at any time. If you receive an email from us and wish to be removed, you can request deletion, and your email address will be permanently removed from our records.
